# Onboarding - the 5-minute path to first reminder

The whole product fails if the first reminder is not sent within 10 minutes of signup.

## Step 1 - Signup (email only)
- No card, no setup, no tour.
- Immediately ask: "What did you invoice last week?" (one field)

## Step 2 - First invoice
- Pre-fill from a guess. Editing is faster than creating.
- Import from CSV for people with history (free tier: max 15 invoices)
- Generate a shareable payment page immediately

## Step 3 - Set the policy
- Defaults: +3 friendly, +7 firm, +14 final
- Show a preview of the three emails BEFORE sending
- "Sounds like you" - let them edit sign-off and business name first

## Step 4 - First reminder
- Send the first reminder immediately (day 0) as a preview to the OWNER, not the client
- "This is what your client will receive" - instant aha moment

## Step 5 - The loop
- Day 1: dashboard shows who owes what
- Day 3: first automatic reminder fires
- Day 7: "your client paid / didn't pay" prompt -> review request

## Activation metric
**Reminder sent >= 1** within 24h of signup. Nothing else counts as activation.

Questions

Does PayPrompt send emails in my name?

Yes. Clients receive a reminder that carries your business name, your sign-off and your payment link. PayPrompt is never presented as the sender.

What happens if a client ignores the reminders?

The sequence stops at the limit you set and the invoice is flagged as "needs attention" in your dashboard. You decide whether to escalate personally or write it off.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — monthly plans, cancel from settings, no cancellation fee. Your invoices and history stay exportable.

Is my financial data safe?

Invoices are encrypted at rest, payment details are handled by Stripe or PayPal and never touch our servers, and we never sell or share client data.

Do I need a Stripe account?

No. Stripe and PayPal are optional — you can send invoices with bank transfer details instead, or connect either one later.

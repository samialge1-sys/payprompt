# PayPrompt - Growth Playbook

Owner: Sami. Execution: Mira. Weekly cadence.

## North star
100 paying customers x $19 = **$1,900 MRR**.

## Funnel (the only 4 numbers that matter)
1. Visitors to landing page
2. Trial starts (email captured)
3. Activated: first invoice + first reminder sent
4. Paid conversion (trial -> paid)

Track weekly. If step 2 -> 3 is weak, the onboarding is broken, not the traffic.

## Channel 1 - Facebook (live)
- Post 2x/week: one problem-post, one build-in-public post
- Always link the landing page
- Reply to every comment within 24h
- Best hook: the "late invoice" pain, not the features

## Channel 2 - X / Twitter (build in public)
- Thread: "how I built a micro-SaaS that chases invoices"
- Daily-ish short posts: MRR updates, screenshots, lessons
- Tag indiehackers, buildinpublic
- Goal: 1,000 followers = distribution channel

## Channel 3 - LinkedIn (B2B, freelancers + agencies)
- Long-form: "freelancers lose 5-15% to late invoices" + the math
- The $2,000/month worked example lands well with agencies
- Goal: 20 agency conversations = 20 trials

## Channel 4 - Reddit (r/freelance, r/entrepreneur, r/SaaS)
- Post the PROBLEM, never a link drop
- "How do you chase overdue invoices without ruining the relationship?"
- Rules-aware: no self-promo, answer first, link only if asked
- Goal: 5 signups from a single good thread

## Channel 5 - Direct outreach (highest ROI, most work)
- 20 agencies/week, personalized: mention a real client of theirs, the exact pain
- Free 2 months for first 10 in exchange for a testimonial + logo
- This is the channel that produces the first $1,000 MRR

## Weekly operating rhythm
- Mon: review metrics, pick the week's single goal
- Tue-Thu: ship + post
- Fri: post results, write next week's content
- Sun: 30-min retro - what moved the needle?

## Pricing & packaging (already live)
Starter $9 / Pro $19 / Agency $39. Flat, no commission on collections.
Agency tier ($39) needs the team/white-label features before it is sellable.

## Conversion rules
- 7-day free trial, no card
- Show the "you lose $100-300/month" math before the feature list
- One CTA per page
- Ask for a testimonial at the moment of first payment, not later
